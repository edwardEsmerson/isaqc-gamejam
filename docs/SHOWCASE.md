# Qubit FC showcase

Use showcase length (two one-minute halves) with Standard mode for a first explanation. The phase-hidden Expert setting is available once players know the gates. Two controllers are preferable; keyboard works with no setup.

## Five-minute live session

1. **30 seconds:** show the globe. North and south are definite outcomes; the hollow gold marker is the target. The arrow's direction sets scoring odds; its length tracks coherence.
2. **90 seconds:** hand the judge a controller and play. A passes; holding B charges a shot. Emphasize that football positioning and physical saves still matter.
3. **60 seconds:** reset through the keeper, then show H → Z → H. Z does not immediately change north-lock odds, but the second H turns its phase change into a full flip.
4. **60 seconds:** switch the defending keeper from Z to X with Y. Switch takes two seconds and freezes in the box. A fresh north ball goes from 100% to 50% when the completed lock changes.
5. **60 seconds:** linger close to a defender and watch the arrow shrink. Passes rotate but cannot repair it. Back-pass to the keeper for a fresh state, then attack again.

## A deliberate hedge

Starting from north, receive at H, T, H, T, Z, T, Z. The arrow ends midway between +X and +Z, and either fixed target gives 85.36%. Extra receptions change the circuit. Under pressure, the odds decrease toward 50%. This is a longer, advanced route; the keeper switch alone already demonstrates incompatible measurement axes.

## Two-minute unedited recording

Use the Standard globe and showcase clock. Keep the recording uncut. Demonstrate a gate combination, keeper switch and pressure; attempt a goal. For a backup demonstration, Watch match runs both tactical teams automatically, with visible gates, saves, measurement failures and match statistics.

Full-time statistics include goals, shots, possession, average shot alignment, rejected measurements, state lost to pressure, keeper saves and passes.
